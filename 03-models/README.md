# Models trained for the BabyLM 2026 Multilingual task

This folder includes the training scripts and all the models trained for the challenge here.

The trained Hawk models selected for the competition are available on Hugging Face [NeTSlab/babylm-2026](https://huggingface.co/collections/NeTSlab/babylm-2026). 
The [eMG-RNN models](https://github.com/cristianochesi/eMG-RNN) (expectation-based, Minimalist Grammar-inspired Recurrent Neural Networks) are included for research purposes only: training them is much less efficient than training Hawk models, although their performance in the BabyLM challenge is comparable.

All models have been debugged and optimized using Claude (Opus 4 and 5)
